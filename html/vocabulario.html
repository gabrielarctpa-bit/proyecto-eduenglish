Vocabulario Técnico

Glosario de términos académicos y vocabulario especializado en inglés.

Analyze
/ˈænəlaɪz/
verb
To examine something carefully in order to understand it or explain its parts.
"The students had to analyze the data before writing their conclusions."
Significant
/sɪɡˈnɪfɪkənt/
adjective
Important enough to be noticed or to have a real effect on something.
"There was a significant improvement in her pronunciation this semester."
Sustainable
/səˈsteɪnəbl/
adjective
Able to continue over time without causing damage to the environment or running out of resources.
"The company switched to sustainable packaging made from recycled paper."
Evidence
/ˈevɪdəns/
noun
Facts, information, or signs that show whether something is true or valid.
"The essay needs more evidence to support the main argument."
Assumption
/əˈsʌmpʃən/
noun
Something you believe to be true without proof or evidence.
"It's a common assumption that older students learn languages more slowly."
Outcome
/ˈaʊtkʌm/
noun
The final result of an activity, process, or decision.
"Researchers were surprised by the outcome of the experiment."
Perspective
/pərˈspektɪv/
noun
A particular way of thinking about or looking at a topic or situation.
"The article offers a new perspective on remote education."
Controversial
/ˌkɒntrəˈvɜːʃəl/
adjective
Causing public disagreement because people have very different opinions about it.
"Artificial intelligence in classrooms is still a controversial topic."
Feasible
/ˈfiːzəbl/
adjective
Possible to do or achieve with the resources and time available.
"Is it feasible to finish the research project by next month?"
